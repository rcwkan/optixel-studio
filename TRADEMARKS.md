## 5. Rules for Forks and Custom Community Distributions

We welcome and encourage forks of Optixel Studio! If you build or distribute a customized version of this software from source:

1. **Mandatory Rebranding**: You must remove all instances of the "Optixel" and "Optixel Studio" name, trademarks, and logos from the user interface, splash screens, About dialogs, and installer wrappers.
2. **Distinct Name**: Choose a unique, distinct name that does not include "Optixel" or suggest endorsement or affiliation by the Optixel Studio project.
3. **Change Bundle Identifiers**: Change all OS-level identifiers (e.g., change `org.optixel.studio` in `tauri.conf.json` and `Cargo.toml` to your own domain and product name).
4. **Separate Update Server**: You must configure your own update infrastructure; community builds may not ping or load assets from official Optixel update endpoints or Cloudflare Worker proxies.
